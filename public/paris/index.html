🇫🇷 Paris

This list is grouped by arrondissement / neighborhood, then by activity — what to see, wander, do, and eat.

1st/2nd — Louvre · Sentier
To See
Bourse de Commerce / Pinault Collection must-go, ticket ahead
Fondation Cartier must-go, ticket ahead, Jean Nouvel, easy to spend 2+ hours
To Wander
Quai des Tuileries walk along the Seine
Rue de Cléry boutiques
Rue Réaumur shopping street
Rue Tiquetonne second-hand clothing
To Eat
Pied de Cochon reserve, classic French, iconic 24hr spot, seafood tower
Au Vieux Comptoir classic French, english-friendly
Tonton Ramen casual, no reservation, €14–18 a bowl
3rd/4th — Marais
To Eat
Chez Janou French bistro, Marais, hard to get reservations
Bofinger reserve, classic Alsatian brasserie, good for bringing in-laws or parents
Bistrot Le Temps des Cerises excellent, fresh produce, no-fuss
5th/13th — Mouffetard · Port-Royal · Alésia
To Wander
Marché Mouffetard farmer/street market — Tue–Sun 8am–1pm, closed Mondays
Port-Royal Market farmer market — Tue & Thu 7am–1:30pm, Sat 7am–2:30pm
Marché Alésia farmer market — Wed 7am–1:30pm, Sat 7am–2:30pm
6th/7th — St Germain · Orsay
To See
Musée d'Orsay must-go, ticket ahead, similar scale to the MET
To Wander
Rue des Saints-Pères boutiques
Jardin du Luxembourg park
Marché Raspail organic/bio market Sun 7am–2:30pm; regular market Tue & Fri
To Do
Mineral and Gem Museum ticketed, affordable, old-school science-lab feel
Le Bon Marché iconic department store, easy to spend an hour
To Eat
Paul Chêne Cherche Midi classic French, generous portions, phone reservation only
Bouillon Chartier Montparnasse inexpensive, classic French, casual
9th — Pigalle · Opéra
To Wander
Rue des Martyrs wander, shops
To Eat
Perception reserve, Korean chef, French-rooted tasting menu, special-occasion, closed Sun/Mon
Pink Mamma known for its pizzas
Shinjuku Pigalle chill, low-key, inexpensive, Japanese
11th/12th — Bastille · Aligre · Oberkampf
To Wander
Marché d'Aligre market
Coulée Verte René-Dumont (Promenade Plantée) must-go, free, elevated park walkway, like Highline in NYC — background on it here
To Eat
Au Passage reserve, lovely and cozy, modern French, sentimental favorite
Bistrot Paul Bert reserve, or try ~30 min before opening for a same-day table, classic bistro, touristy but solid
Kodawari Ramen casual
Chez Elisa casual, chef is Hong Kong-born and raised, fun twist on French food
14th/15th — Montparnasse · Cambronne
To Wander
Rue du Commerce wander
To Do
Émile Anthoine Stadium great place to run, views of the Eiffel Tower
To Eat
Pho Tai Michelin-starred, Vietnamese
Le Café du Commerce reserve, good for bringing in-laws, food is fine but not exceptional
MELT Cambronne Texas BBQ, excellent and surprisingly good
Partage Café non-French brunch, good
Caractère de Cochon cold cut store, good for a quick bite
Le Petit Vendôme sandwiches, French, €10–40
Rouge & Blanc Boulangerie Pâtisserie good pastries
16th — Bois de Boulogne
To See
Fondation Louis Vuitton (Gehry) ticket ahead, modern architecture
Musée d'Art Moderne de Paris must-go, free permanent collection, modern art
To Wander
Bois de Boulogne run, park
18th/19th — Saint-Ouen · La VilletteAntique market
To See
Cité des Sciences et de l'Industrie great visit with kids
To Wander
Marché aux Puces de Saint-Ouen flea market, open Sat/Sun/Mon only
Quai de l'Oise along the canal — playground, canal-side cafés and bars
