←⬜️ Le Corbusier Trail

16th, 14th, 13th, and 7th arrondissements
See the Fondation Le Corbusier for open dates.

A cluster of cultural landmarks worth weaving in if there's a gap, especially given the interest in living in Paris again. Access varies a lot from site to site, some are proper museums, some are exterior-only.

16th — Le Corbusier’s studio apartment

His own home and studio, in the Molitor building. Open Thu, Fri, Sat only, limited hours. Book ahead through the Fondation Le Corbusier.

16th — Fondation Le Corbusier / Maison La Roche

Closed Sundays. Can be combined with the studio apartment on a twin ticket. Both sit right by the Bois de Boulogne, easy to pair with your Tuesday there.

14th — Fondation Suisse / Pavillon Le Corbusier

Inside Cité Internationale Universitaire. Guided visits of the entrance hall and lounge.

14th — Maison du Brésil

Right across from the Pavillon Suisse. Exterior always viewable, interior by guided tour only.

Boulogne-Billancourt — Villa Cook

Just outside Paris proper. Private residence, exterior view only.

14th — Maison Ozenfant

Near Parc Montsouris. Cultural landmark, now a private residence, exterior only.

13th — Cité de Refuge

1933. Salvation Army building, his first major Paris work. Guided tours or during exhibitions only, check the Fondation Le Corbusier site for open dates.

7th — Maison de Verre

Near Saint-Germain-des-Prés. Worth noting, this one is actually by Pierre Chareau, not Le Corbusier, but built the same years and part of the same modernist moment. Private, visits by appointment only, roughly one Thursday a month, 40€, book months ahead.
